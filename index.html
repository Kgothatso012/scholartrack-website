02 — Features

Built for
Every Role.

ScholarTrack has three dedicated interfaces — for parents, drivers, and school admins. Each one designed for exactly what that person needs to do.

FEAT — 001
📍
Real-Time GPS Tracking
See your child's bus on a live map updated every 10 seconds. Know exactly when it leaves, where it is right now, and when it arrives. No guessing. No calling.
FEAT — 002
🆘
Emergency SOS
One button. Instantly alerts school admin, parents, and emergency services with the bus's live location.
FEAT — 003
🔔
Push Notifications
Parents receive automatic alerts when the bus departs, is 5 minutes away, and when their child is dropped off.
FEAT — 004
✅
Digital Attendance
Drivers tap to mark students on/off. Records are instant and permanent.
FEAT — 005
🗺️
Route Replays
Admins can replay any trip. Full audit trail of every route, every day.
FEAT — 006
📊
Admin Dashboard
All buses, all routes, all students — one real-time command centre for the school.
FEAT — 007
🔒
POPIA Compliant
All child data encrypted and stored in accordance with South African privacy law.
FEAT — 008
📱
Works on Any Phone
Progressive web app — no app store needed. Parents, drivers, and admins use it in their browser. Designed for South African data realities: lightweight, fast on 3G, and it works offline.
