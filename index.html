03 — Emergency SOS

One Button.
Total Peace of Mind.

When a driver hits the SOS button, the system springs into immediate action. The driver's live GPS location is broadcast to school admin, all parents of children on that bus, and SA emergency services — all within seconds.

1
Driver presses SOS — the button is prominently placed in the driver app, designed for instant access in an emergency.
2
Live location broadcast — the bus's current GPS coordinates are sent to the school admin dashboard and all emergency contacts simultaneously.
3
Emergency services called — ScholarTrack automatically calls 10111 with the bus location and student manifest. No manual dialing needed.
4
Parents notified — every parent of a child on that bus receives an immediate push notification and SMS with the situation and location.
