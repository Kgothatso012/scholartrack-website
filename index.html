The "Where's the Bus?" Call
Parents call the school. The school calls the driver. Nobody knows. This happens hundreds of times per school, every week.
No Emergency Protocol
A bus breaks down or a child feels unsafe. There is no button to press, no alert to send. The driver must stop and find a phone.
