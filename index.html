03 — Emergency SOS

One Button.
Total Peace of Mind.

When a driver hits SOS, the system springs into immediate action. Live GPS broadcast to school admin, all parents, and SA emergency services — all within seconds.

1
Driver presses SOS — prominently placed in the driver app for instant access in an emergency.
2
Live location broadcast — bus GPS sent to school admin dashboard and all emergency contacts simultaneously.
3
Emergency services called — ScholarTrack auto-calls 10111 with bus location and student manifest. No manual dialing.
4
Parents notified — immediate push notification and SMS to every parent of a child on that bus.
