ScholarTrack Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 9 August 2026

ScholarTrack ("we", "us", "the Operator") operates the MalumeScholarTrack

mobile application ("the App"), a school-transport coordination service that

connects parents with drivers and shares live trip location for child safety.

This policy explains what personal information we process, why, and the rights

you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) and the

Constitution of the Republic of South Africa.

By creating an account you consent to the processing described here. You can

withdraw consent at any time (see Your rights). The App records your

consent_version; if we update this policy we will ask you to accept the new

version before you continue using the App.

2. Personal information we collect

Account information: full name, email address, mobile number, and your

role (parent / driver / admin).

If you are a parent: your children's first name, school, grade, and the

parent–child link; emergency contacts (name, phone, relationship); and trip

records (assigned driver, child, pickup/dropoff addresses and coordinates,

scheduled and actual times, status).

If you are a driver: RSA ID number, driving licence number, Professional

Driving Permit (PDP) number, vehicle details, and compliance documents; your

availability and trip assignments.

Precise location: while a trip is in progress the App collects precise

geographical location from the driver's device and shares it with the child's

parent and the assigned driver so the parent can see the child's position and

receive arrival (geofence) alerts. Location is collected for the driver while a

trip is active, including when the App is in the background, and is not

collected outside active trips.

Safety events: SOS / panic alerts, geofence entry/exit events, and the

location at which they were raised.

Payments: payment amount, status and reference. Card data is handled by

Paystack; we do not store full card numbers.

Device and usage: Expo push-notification token, device type, and

service-usage logs needed to operate and secure the App.

3. Why we process it (purpose and legal basis)

  • To provide the service (contract): matching parents and drivers,

    • scheduling trips, and recording trip history.

  • For child safety (consent and legitimate interest): live location

    • sharing during trips, geofence arrival alerts, and SOS.

  • For driver verification and compliance (legal obligation): validating

    • RSA ID numbers and driver credentials.

  • To process payments (contract): through Paystack.
  • To keep the App secure and functioning (legitimate interest): error

    • monitoring, fraud prevention, and service reliability.

4. Children's personal information

The App's purpose is children's transport. Children's data (name, school,

location during a trip) is provided and consented to by the parent or legal

guardian. We process it only to deliver the trip and the safety features. A

child's location is visible only to that child's parent, the assigned driver,

and authorised administrators. We do not market to children and do not create

behavioural profiles of children.

5. Sharing and recipients

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed to operate:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting (responsible processor).
  • Google — Maps and Places APIs for addresses and routing.
  • Paystack — payment processing.
  • Expo — push notification delivery.

Each processor processes data under instructions from us and is bound to

protect it. Some may process data outside South Africa; we rely on standard

contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards for any cross-border transfer.

We may disclose information where required by law (for example a lawful request

from the South African Police Service or a court order).

6. Retention

  • Active account data: while your account is active.
  • Live driver location (driver_tracking): during the active trip and for

    • 90 days afterwards, then deleted.

  • Trip records: 12 months after completion.
  • Payment records: 5 years (financial-record-keeping obligations).
  • Marketing leads: 6 months, then deleted.
  • Consent records: for the life of the account.
  • Deleted-account audit: we keep a permanent record that an account was

    • deleted (user id, role, email, date) to meet our accountability

    obligations; no account contents are retained after deletion.

7. Security

We protect personal information with row-level security on the database,

encryption in transit (TLS), separation of the public (anon) key from

service-role access, and least-privilege access. Only the Information Officer

and authorised administrators can access the backend. If a security breach

likely to affect your rights occurs, we will notify affected data subjects and

the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

8. Your rights (POPIA)

You may, free of charge and at reasonable intervals:

  • Access your data — in the App: **Settings → Data & Privacy → Export my

    • data** (returns your profile, children, trips, emergency contacts, payments,

    tracking and consents as JSON).

  • Correct inaccurate data — via your profile or by contacting the

    • Information Officer.

  • Delete (erasure) — Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete my account.

    • This permanently erases your data and removes your account; an audit record

    of the deletion is kept.

  • Object to processing for legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time — withdraw location consent in Settings or

    • delete your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing before withdrawal.

  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if you are not

    • satisfied with our response.

To exercise any right, email privacy@scholartrack.co.za.

version (the CONSENT_VERSION is bumped) before you continue. The current

version is always available at scholartrack.co.za/privacy.
